Apple Lisa ROMs
===============

BMLisa needs the ROMs of the Lisa it emulates. They are copyrighted by
Apple, so they are not included: copy your own into this folder.

Lisa 2 (Lisa 2/5, Lisa 2/10, and the Macintosh XL; the usual choice)
  341-0175-h     the boot ROM's high byte chip, 8K
  341-0176-h     the boot ROM's low byte chip, 8K
  vidstate.rom   the video state machine's PROM, 256 bytes

Lisa 1 (two Twiggy drives)
  L175REVC.bin   the boot ROM's high byte chip, 8K
  L176REVC.bin   the boot ROM's low byte chip, 8K
  vidstate.rom   as for the Lisa 2

These are the names IDLE, the emulator BMLisa runs, uses. MAME's lisa2.zip
has the Lisa 2's under them: copy 341-0175-h, 341-0176-h and vidstate.rom
out of the zip file into this folder.

If both are there, BMLisa starts the Lisa 2. Choose the other under Machine
in the F12 menu.
